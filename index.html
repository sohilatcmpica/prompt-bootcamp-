Detailed Curriculum

10–15 Hours of Action-Packed Learning

01

What is Prompt Engineering?

Theme: Asking is a skill • 1.5 Hours

Discover what AI really does (and doesn't do) and why clarity of thought matters more than technology. Activity: Same question, different prompts.

02

How AI Thinks

Theme: Machine Mindset • 2 Hours
Learn about language processing, context, and intent. Identify why vague prompts fail and how structure guides the model.
03

The Prompt Framework

Theme: Structure brings Power • 2.5 Hours
Master Formula

Role → Task → Context → Constraints → Output

Students rewrite basic prompts using the framework and participate in peer reviews.

04

Prompting for Students

Theme: Study smarter, not harder • 2.5 Hours
Build a "Study Prompt Toolkit". Use AI for understanding difficult topics, making summaries, and preparing for exams.
05

Creative Prompting

Theme: From imagination to output • 3 Hours
Story creation, idea generation, and problem-solving. Mini-project: Create a study planner or presentation outline using AI.
06

Ethics & Responsible Use

Theme: Smart users think critically • 1.5 Hours
Hallucinations, bias, and plagiarism. Learn when NOT to use AI and how to fact-check AI responses.
07

Final Challenge

Theme: Demonstration of skill • 2 Hours
Design a prompt for a real-world problem, iterate through improvements, and present the final output.

Measurable Outcomes

01

Write clear, structured prompts that get accurate results every time.

02

Identify and correct AI hallucinations and misleading responses.

03

Use AI as a learning assistant for studies, presentations, and exam prep.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need any prior coding knowledge?
No coding is required! Prompt Engineering is about logic, structure, and communication. It's accessible to anyone in any stream.
What tools will I learn?
We primarily use ChatGPT, but we also cover Google Gemini, Claude, and Perplexity to show the differences in how they think and respond.
Will I get a certificate?
Yes! You will receive a Certificate of Completion in Prompt Engineering Fundamentals upon finishing all modules and the final challenge.
